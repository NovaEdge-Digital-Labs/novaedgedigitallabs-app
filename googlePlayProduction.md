## Closed Test Production Access Answers

**Question 1: How did you recruit users for your closed test?**
I reached out to freelancers, students, job seekers, and small business owners in my professional network. I personally onboarded them to test project posting, job applications, course viewing, and escrow checkout flows.

**Question 2: How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?**
Easy

**Question 3: Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test.**
Testers explored all key product personas (Clients posting jobs/gigs, Freelancers submitting proposals, Students taking courses, Job seekers applying to openings). Engagement was high, with multiple active transactions and course views logged.

**Question 4: Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you collected the feedback.**
Collected via direct messages and structured Google Forms. Testers praised the clean dark-glass UI and the multi-role onboarding experience. Based on feedback, we added explicit Escrow safety badges and improved search performance with request debouncing.

**Question 5: Who is the intended audience of your app?**
Freelancers, tech clients/businesses, job seekers, employers, and tech students looking for freelance opportunities, job listings, coding education, and developer tools in India.

**Question 6: Describe how your app provides value to users.**
It connects employers with freelancers using secure Escrow protection, provides job seekers with remote career opportunities, and offers accessible tech education and digital productivity tools in a single app.

**Question 7: How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?**
0 - 10K

**Question 8: What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?**
We added persona-based onboarding screens, implemented 400ms search debouncing, integrated full Escrow trust badges across checkout & proposal flows, and aligned all Academy screens to a unified dark theme.

**Question 9: How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**
All core flows (escrow contracts, Razorpay payments, job listings, course playback, persona routing) passed testing without crashes or data discrepancies across multiple test devices.
