## 📌 Role Overview

NovaEdge Digital Labs is looking for a creative mind with a keen eye for detail to join us as a **UI/UX Design Intern**. In this role, you will work on designing intuitive user interfaces and crafting smooth, engaging user experiences for real-world digital products. 

Whether you are looking to build your professional portfolio, gain hands-on experience, or collaborate with experienced developers, this internship provides a supportive environment to sharpen your design skills.

---

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

- **Wireframing & Prototyping:** Assist in creating wireframes, interactive prototypes, and clear user flow diagrams.
- **Developer Collaboration:** Work closely with developers to ensure UI designs are accurately translated into working digital interfaces.
- **User Research & Usability:** Conduct basic user research, competitive analysis, and usability testing to iterate on design solutions.
- **Design Consistency:** Maintain design consistency across web and mobile platforms using UI component libraries and design tokens.

---

## 🎓 Qualifications & Requirements

- **No Degree Required:** We value skills and passion—just bring a portfolio or design samples that speak for themselves.
- **Tool Proficiency:** Familiarity with modern design tools such as **Figma**, **Adobe XD**, or similar software.
- **Design Fundamentals:** A solid understanding of core design principles, typography, visual hierarchy, and color theory.
- **Communication & Curiosity:** Strong eagerness to learn, accept constructive feedback, and collaborate effectively in a remote team setting.
